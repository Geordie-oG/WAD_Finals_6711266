## Steps after implementation

1. Open the frontend deployment above. Test credentials are in README.md; all six accounts use `Learn2026!`.
2. Log in as Teacher 1 (`teacher1@courses.test`). Create an Open course with several topics. View/edit it.
3. Log out and log in as Student 1 (`student1@courses.test`). Search its course name or `Alex`, open the details, enroll, then open My learning. Refresh to demonstrate persistence.
4. Return as Teacher 1. Open the course and show the enrolled Student roster and disabled deletion.
5. Use a course with Max students 1 to demonstrate that another Student cannot enroll after it becomes full.
6. Show that Close courses remain visible but cannot accept enrollments and Cancel courses are hidden from Students.
7. Log in as Teacher 2 (`teacher2@courses.test`) and show only that Teacher's own courses.
8. Show the backend health URL: https://wad-finals-6711266-backend.vercel.app/api/health.
9. Confirm the GitHub repository contains the two source folders and README test credentials. Secrets and the paper are excluded.
10. Keep the required recording running through the examination. When finished, upload it, check its viewing permissions, and submit its link with the four source/deployment links above.

Do not submit only the repository homepage: provide both folder links requested by the paper. Both source folders are in one exam repository.
